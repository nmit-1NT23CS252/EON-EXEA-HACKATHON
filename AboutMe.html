◆ What makes you want to build like a polymath?

Building like a polymath isn't about collecting titles; it’s about synthesis. Real-world problems don't exist in neat academic silos. To build truly impactful products—whether it's combining AI computer vision with real-time fleet logistics or blending software engineering with physical product design—you have to understand the entire stack. Engineering provides structural rigor, design grants human empathy, and curiosity connects the dots. I build as a polymath because breakthrough innovation happens at the intersection of disciplines: where deep technical backend systems meet seamless human experiences. Mastering multiple domains allows me to turn cross-disciplinary ideas into tangible reality.
